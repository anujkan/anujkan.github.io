CricGuide – Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · Developer: NextFrame Media Studio · App: CricGuide (com.nextframe.cricguide) · About the app

Summary

CricGuide is a free cricket TV guide for India, Pakistan and Bangladesh. It has no account and no sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, phone number, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. The developer does not collect personal data. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes. These Google services may collect some device information, as described below.

Match schedule download

The app downloads a public schedule file (the match list and where to watch each match) from anujkan.github.io/cricguide/data.json, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. This file is the same for everyone and the app sends nothing about you when it downloads it. As with any website, GitHub may log your IP address for security and operation of its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app checks for a new file now and then in the background so that reminders and the home-screen widget stay up to date.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your settings on your device only: language, country, the teams you follow, the channels and apps you choose under "My apps", theme, reminder time and the reminders you set. It also keeps the last downloaded schedule so the app works without internet. To suggest your country on first launch, the app reads the country code of your SIM card or phone region on the device; this is not sent to the developer. All of this is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Reminders and notifications

CricGuide shows a notification only for a match reminder you set, or for matches of your followed teams if you turn on automatic reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, and the app asks for notification permission (Android 13 and later) only when you first tap "Remind me". The app does not send marketing notifications.

Ads (Google AdMob)

CricGuide uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information such as device model, Android version, app version and language,
  • information about how ads are shown and interacted with, and diagnostic information.

Google may use this to show personalised or non-personalised ads, to prevent fraud and to measure ad performance. The developer does not receive this data in identifiable form and does not sell it. To learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires consent for ads, such as in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the app asks for it using Google's consent tool before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation on your phone under Settings → Google → Ads (menu names vary by phone).

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which features are useful and fix problems, the app sends anonymous usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase:

  • which features you use: finishing setup, filters, changing country, tapping a channel or app, setting a reminder, adding a match to your calendar, opening the live score, reporting wrong info and adding a widget,
  • simple details of those actions, such as the language and country chosen, the number of teams followed and whether a tapped app is installed,
  • whether the schedule download worked and how old the data is,
  • standard app events recorded by Firebase: first open, sessions, time spent in the app, app updates and uninstalls,
  • device and app information such as device model, Android version, app version, country and an app-instance identifier created by Firebase.

The app does not send your name, phone number, email, contacts or exact location. The developer sees only totals and trends in the Firebase console and does not sell this data. Google processes it as a service provider under the Google Privacy Policy; see also firebase.google.com/support/privacy. Detailed analytics data is kept for 2 months (the Google Analytics default); only summary reports are kept longer.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to help fix it. A report contains technical details only: what went wrong in the app's code, device model, Android version, app version, and an identifier for the app installation created by Crashlytics. It does not contain your name, contacts or messages. Google processes this as a service provider; crash reports are kept for 90 days. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Other apps and websites

Some buttons open other apps or websites: a streaming app or its Google Play page, a Google search for the live score, your calendar app to add a match, and your email app for "Report wrong info" or "Report a problem". CricGuide does not read your calendar or email. What happens in those apps and sites is covered by their own privacy policies. If you send us an email, we receive your email address and what you write, and use it only to reply and to fix the information.

Children

CricGuide is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Accuracy of information

Match times and broadcasters can change. CricGuide is a guide only; it does not stream matches. Please check with the broadcaster if something looks wrong, and tell us using "Report wrong info".

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. No other analytics or tracking code is included; if this changes, this policy will be updated before the new version is released.
